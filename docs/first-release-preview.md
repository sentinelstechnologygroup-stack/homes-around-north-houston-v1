Campaign query parameters survive search/property navigation and are forwarded with the inquiry. The weekly preference is a request for manual setup; no recurring listing emails are enabled. Cross-device visitor accounts and advanced saved-search controls are reserved for the fuller Golden Cross experience.

The existing GMS route is configured for this branch and remains **paused**. Inquiries cannot claim success without a confirmed receipt, and no live call or Realtor handoff is initiated by this preview. Search Homes links open `/homes` directly; the contact popup intercepts only contact links. Search/property inquiries carry their criteria into capture and optional qualification; general contacts collect preferences separately. The contact step has one brokerage/office-support checkbox and no SMS opt-in. SMS permission is always false. Footer technology disclosures remain in place.

No paid map, email, SMS or Repliers add-on was added. OpenStreetMap tiles are for modest preview testing with visible attribution; production campaign usage requires a hosting/capacity review. Repliers Standard does not include its email/listing-alert delivery features in the published plan comparison. Recurring alerts, suppression/unsubscribe, client review, full controlled handoff, conversion tracking acceptance and production rate limiting still need completion before launch.
